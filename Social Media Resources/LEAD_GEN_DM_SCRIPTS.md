```

"Hey! Thanks for reaching out to Calcutta Node 🙌

We build websites, apps, and help businesses grow online — all at affordable prices with 24/7 support.

To give you the best quote, can you tell me:
1. What do you need? (Website / App / SEO / Design / Other)
2. Your budget range
3. When you need it

I'll get back with a fixed price within 2 hours. Always."
```

### When They Ask "Is It Really This Cheap?"

```
Totally fair question! Here's why our pricing is so good:

1. We work remotely — no office rent
2. We're based in Hooghly (not Mumbai/Delhi) — lower costs, same quality
3. We use proven open-source tools & modern frameworks
4. We don't outsource — I do everything personally or with a small trusted team
5. We want long-term clients, not one-time customers

I've delivered 36+ projects and 99% of our clients refer us or come back.

Want to see some work samples? I can send them right now 📸

Also — WELCOME500 gets you ₹500 off your first booking if you'd like to try us out!
```

### When They Say "I Need to Think About It"

```
No pressure at all! That's completely fine 👍

Here's what I'd suggest:
1. Save this conversation for when you're ready
2. Check out our website: calcuttanode.vercel.app
3. DM me anytime — no rush, no deadline

And if you change your mind or want a free website audit first, just say the word. I'll do it no strings attached.

Either way, good luck with your project! 🚀
```

### When They've Seen Your Portfolio and Want to Proceed

```
That's great to hear! 🎉

Here's how we move forward:
1. I'll send you a fixed price quote (no surprises)
2. 50% advance payment to start
3. We deliver — with updates along the way
4. Final delivery + 50% balance
5. Ongoing support if you need it

Payment methods: Razorpay, UPI, or direct bank transfer — whatever works for you.

Want me to put together the quote for your specific project? Just tell me more about what you need 👇
```

### When They're Confused About Which Service

```
No worries — let's figure this out together! 🤝

Quick questions:
1. What's the goal? (Sell more online? Look professional? Get found on Google? Get a mobile app?)
2. Who's the target audience? (Customers? Local clients? Other businesses?)
3. What's been done so far? (Nothing? A basic website? Something that's not working?)

Based on your answers, I'll recommend the right service and price — no guesswork, no generic packages.

Just tell me what you're trying to do and I'll guide you! 🧭
```
